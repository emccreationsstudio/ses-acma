```
Her tur bir ton akoruyla başlar. Ardından seçtiğin egzersiz çalar ve her turda yarım ton yükselir; istersen tepeden geri iner. Söylemen gereken heceler ekranda yazar, çalan notalar klavyede yanar.

DOĞRU NOTADA MISIN?
```

**Description (4000)**
```
Ses Açma is a piano-accompanied vocal warm-up app for singing students, singers and choir members. Get your voice ready in a few minutes before lessons, choir rehearsals or a performance.

HOW IT WORKS
Each round starts with a chord. Then the exercise plays and moves up a half step every round, and can come back down from the top if you like. The syllables to sing are shown on screen and the notes light up on the keyboard.

6 READY-MADE EXERCISES
• Humming – a gentle start to wake up the voice
• Five-Note Ladder – the classic five-note scale on Mee-May-Mah-Moh-Moo
• Staccato – short, light notes that engage the breath
• Arpeggio – up to the octave through the chord
• Octave Leap – sing wide intervals with confidence
• Nine-Note Scale – a long scale for advanced students
Every card shows a small curve of the melody, so you can see the shape of the exercise at a glance.

ARE YOU ON THE NOTE?
Turn on the microphone and the app listens as you sing, guiding you with "Spot on!", "A little higher" or "A little lower". At the end, see how many notes you hit with stars. Octaves don't matter: singing an octave lower also counts as correct.

FITS EVERY VOICE
• Voice types: Child, Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass
• Set the starting and highest notes by half steps
• Tempo: 60–160 BPM
• Up only, or up and down
• Turn off the melody: only the chord plays and students sing by ear
• Tap any key to hear a note

FOR TEACHERS
Starting a lesson with a warm-up takes one tap. Put your phone next to the piano, pick a voice type and press Start. The screen stays on during the exercise.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking. The app collects no personal data. The microphone is used only when you turn it on; sound is analysed on your phone in real time, never recorded and never sent anywhere. No internet connection needed.

Available in English and Turkish.
```

## İnceleme notu (App Review Information → Notes, İngilizce yazın)
```
Ses Açma plays piano-accompanied vocal warm-up exercises. No login or account is required.

To test: choose an exercise and voice type, then tap Start. The piano plays a chord, then the exercise, rising a half step each round.

The microphone is optional: turn on "Listen with microphone" in Settings to see real-time pitch feedback while singing. Audio is analysed on-device only and is never recorded or transmitted. The app works fully offline and contains no in-app purchases, ads or third-party SDKs.
```

## Fiyat önerisi
Benzer ses ısınma uygulamaları genelde 2–5 USD bandında. Başlangıç için **2,99 USD** seviyesini seçip Türkiye fiyatını Apple'ın otomatik çevirisine bırakabilirsiniz; App Store Connect > Fiyatlandırma ve Kullanılabilirlik'ten sonra her an değiştirilebilir.

Satıştan önce App Store Connect > **İş (Business)** bölümünde **Ücretli Uygulamalar Sözleşmesi**, banka ve vergi bilgileri tamamlanmalı; yoksa ücretli uygulama yayınlanmaz. Yıllık geliri 1 milyon USD altındaki geliştiriciler **App Store Small Business Program**'a başvurarak komisyonu %15'e düşürebilir.
